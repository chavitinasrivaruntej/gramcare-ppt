GramCare

GramCare

GramCare is a rural healthcare access platform designed to bridge the gap between underserved village populations and qualified urban doctors through an assisted teleconsultation model. Instead of relying on direct digital usage, GramCare operates through local healthcare centers where trained community admins manage the entire consultation process for patients. This ensures that even individuals without smartphones or digital literacy can access quality medical care.

By providing doctors with structured patient data before consultations and enabling continuous communication even in low-network conditions, GramCare makes healthcare more accessible, reliable, and efficient. The platform focuses on building trust through local presence while using technology to deliver timely and affordable medical support to rural communities.

SYSTEM WORKFLOW

HOW GRAMCARE WORKS

1. Patient Arrival

Patient visits the local health center with their health concerns.

2. Details Captured

Trained community admin notes down symptoms, vitals, and medical history into the platform.

3. Appointment Booked

The system automatically schedules a slot with the right urban specialist.

4. Online Consultation

Doctor reviews structured data and conducts a video/audio consultation guided by the admin.

5. Follow-up Checkups

Post-consultation care, prescriptions, and follow-ups are managed locally to ensure recovery.
